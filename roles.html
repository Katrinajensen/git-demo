Where to look

A mix of MBA-specific boards, firm career pages, and general boards filtered to strategy/commercial titles.

  • MBA-focused boards: your school's career portal, Poets&Quants job board, MBA Exchange, Konsultify (consulting-specific).
  • Big-3 & MBB-adjacent: McKinsey, BCG, Bain career sites directly — many roles never get posted elsewhere.
  • Big 4 & advisory strategy arms: Deloitte S&O, Accenture Strategy, PwC Strategy&, EY-Parthenon.
  • In-house strategy & commercial teams: corporate strategy, S&O, or "office of the CEO" roles at large tech, CPG, retail, and healthcare companies — search their careers page directly, not just LinkedIn.
  • Marketing & growth: brand management at CPG firms, growth/marketing manager roles at scale-up tech companies.
  • General boards, filtered: LinkedIn and Handshake, with saved searches (below) rather than browsing the firehose.

Search terms that actually surface these roles

Titles vary a lot by company — rotate through these instead of just "strategy."
